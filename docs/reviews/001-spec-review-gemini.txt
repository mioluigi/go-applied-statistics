DR-5: Floating-point parsing and summation order at 𝑛 = 100, 000
(Spec §6, Risk R2)
 ●​ Severity: Medium
 ●​ Where: specs/001-bootstrap-median/spec.md §6 ("Sample mean | relative difference ≤
    1e-12" and "Bootstrap SE of the mean | relative difference ≤ 1e-12") and §7 R2 ("R and Go
    may parse the same decimal text to different doubles | Values are written with 4 decimals
    (few significant digits parse exactly in both)").
 ●​ Problem:
 1.​ Risk R2's explanation ("few significant digits parse exactly in both") is technically
     inaccurate: 4-decimal numbers like 0.0001 are infinite repeating fractions in binary
     (0.0001 is not a dyadic rational), so they do not represent exactly in float64. Rather, both
     R's R_strtod and Go's strconv.ParseFloat guarantee correct rounding to the nearest
     IEEE-754 binary64 value (0.5 ULP), which guarantees that both languages parse any
     4-decimal string to the identical 64-bit IEEE-754 bit pattern.
