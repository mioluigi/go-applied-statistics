Notes on T09:
- The program was written before its tests (a lapse from test-first); the mutation checks are the evidence
  that the tests fail without the behaviour.
- **The mutation check itself was wrong at first.** Every mutant "survived" because `go test` answered from
  its result cache. Re-run with `-count=1`, five were caught and one really survived (now closed). All
  mutation runs use `-count=1` from here on. Earlier "caught" results are unaffected: Go never caches a
  failing run, so the cache can only produce a false "survived".

Note (after review R2): the goroutine test failed once in about ten full runs under the race detector,
with nothing leaked. Cause: it counted goroutines the instant the engine returned, and a worker is still
counted for a moment after it reports its work done. The test now waits up to two seconds for the count to
return; the leak mutation above is still caught. The engine was not changed.
